# The Art of Possibility

An independent practice companion for the book by Rosamund Stone Zander and
Benjamin Zander. A reader arrives with a real situation, finds a useful idea,
and leaves with one action to try. The site teaches all twelve practices in
original language and identifies its situations and exercises as original.

Home demonstrates how the same facts can lead to different next moves. The
collection supports book order, search, and three reading paths created for this
guide. Every lesson provides an explanation, imagined situation, three-step
exercise, expected output, important distinction, and reflection prompt.

Success means a useful experiment, not completing a curriculum. Guided mode is
optional, reversible, and controlled by the reader. No streaks or scores.

The notebook stays in the browser with explicit saving, export, and deletion.
No accounts, analytics, external fonts, or note uploads. Existing version-one
notes remain compatible. Static HTML provides reading and exercises without JS.

The standalone dev, build, and test commands remain. Root and subdirectory
hosting use relative URLs. Scope is entirely this directory.
