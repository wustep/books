# Design

A practical book companion with the feel of a working reading copy. Cream paper,
forest ink, and citron marks keep the page quiet and the choices visible.

Literata carries headlines and explanations. Bricolage carries interface copy.
Small monospaced labels distinguish navigation and estimates from teaching.
Both bundled fonts are local and retain their OFL licenses.

The home pairs a large question with a concrete frame experiment. A dark panel
offers three problem-based starting points. All twelve practices remain visible
in a ruled reading index. Lesson pages pair a reading rail with a limited text
measure; the exercise has its own paper tone and a clear output.

Motion is reader-triggered and stage-driven. `public/motion.js` includes the
storyboard, timing constants, spring configurations, cancellation, and reduced
motion. Physical spring responses are sampled into native Web Animations.
There is no animation library, automatic carousel, or debug tuner.

Guided mode shows one instruction at a time; the overview, print view, and
no-JavaScript version keep all three. Native select, buttons, details, and dialog
provide accessible interaction conventions. Notebook saving is explicit.

See `CRAFT.md` for the baseline critique, three Interface Craft passes, visual
pass, three content passes, re-critiques, and validation record.
